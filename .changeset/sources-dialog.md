Sources opens in a dialog instead of a panel docked beside the dashboard. With `enableProvenance`, the bar's Sources button puts the dashboard in inspect mode and now looks clearly pressed: a filled background, a thin ring and an accent dot, with `aria-pressed` and a new `data-state="on" | "off"` to restyle it. An All queries button appears beside it. Hovering a number, table or chart outlines it and, after a short pause, shows a hover card with its source kind, the query's first line and a one-line result (also on keyboard focus; not on touch screens). Tab reaches every item marked with `bindSource()`, and Enter or Space opens it. A click opens a centered 560px dialog on that item, titled by its label with a picked value beside it, a bottom sheet at 639px and below, with the same list, Query, Result, Formula, copy and download as before. Closing it keeps inspect mode and gives focus back to the picked item; the first Escape closes the dialog and the second leaves inspect mode, moving focus to the Sources toggle.

`useArtifactProvenance()` adds `isSourcesOpen` and `openList()`, so a custom toggle can offer All queries, and `setInspecting(false)` also closes the dialog and clears the selection. The renderer's root carries `data-openui-inspecting` while inspecting. `Provenance` children now render inside the open dialog, under its title, back and close. `describeSource` names your tools in both the dialog and the hover card, and the `--openui-inspect-*` variables, `[data-openui-hover]` and `[data-openui-selected]` work as before. New class names: `openui-data-sources-dialog` (with `__overlay`, `__header`, `__title`, `__meta`, `__back`, `__close` and `__custom`), `openui-data-sources-hover-card`, `openui-data-sources-panel__list-button`, `openui-artifact-all-queries` and `openui-artifact-provenance-dot`. The dock's class names `openui-data-sources-workspace` and `openui-data-sources-slot` are removed. Hosts that styled them, or that relied on `Provenance` children rendering inline beside the dashboard, need to update.
