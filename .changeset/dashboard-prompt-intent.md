The prompt options now decide what a dashboard shows before how it looks. Nine design rules come first: start from what the reader came to find out, order the page as the answer, then what explains it, then detail, keep the top of the page varied, show measures that explain each other together, open filters on what was asked, say a period in progress once, write plain titles and labels, and pick each component from the whole library. The group notes follow the same page order, with notes on layout, Kpi charts, table column kinds and list limits, and the three examples are new: a change to explain, a target to track and work to prioritise. Small functional rules cover Sections missing from `root`, `delta` column formats, markers at a chart's edge and plucks after `@Filter`.
